Maintainable code makes the next change less surprising.

## A practical checklist

- Name things after the domain, not the implementation detail.
- Keep effects near the edge of the system.
- Make invalid states difficult to represent.
- Leave a test or a clear manual verification path for behavior that matters.

Clarity compounds: a small amount of deliberate structure today can remove a large amount of uncertainty later.
